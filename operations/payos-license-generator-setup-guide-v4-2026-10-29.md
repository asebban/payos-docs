# payos-license-generator setup guide (obsolete — see v7)

This v4 guide named the executable jar as `payos-license-generator-<version>.jar` and described the validating side as a file-presence check only. After `payos-license-generator`'s shade-plugin classifier fix, the executable jar is `payos-license-generator-<version>-standalone.jar`; `payos-kernel`'s `LicenseValidator` now performs full cryptographic validation.
